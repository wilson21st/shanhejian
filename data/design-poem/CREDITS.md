# 背景音乐 · music (v2)

Excerpts of Wilson's own downloads in `data/bgm/` (the same files the map page plays), cut to 2–2½ minutes, loudness-normalised
to −20 LUFS, faded at both ends, mono 96 kbps, in `bgm/`. **These recordings are not free-licensed**: fine for this private mock,
to be replaced (or licensed) before anything is published.

- **bgm/baidi.mp3** (早发白帝城, light) — 自得琴社《长安幻世绘》 (古琴, 筝, 笛, 鼓), 3:59–6:29 of `data/bgm/changan.mp3` (v3.1: moved
  from the slow, dark 0:20–2:50 opening to the bright, quick 筝/笛 section).
- **bgm/chunwang.mp3** (春望, grief) — 自得琴社《桂枝香》 (on 王安石《桂枝香·金陵怀古》, a lament for a fallen capital), its quiet
  opening 0:15–2:15 of `data/bgm/guizhixiang.mp3` — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OA8YIEo7is
- **bgm/huanghelou.mp3** (黄鹤楼, wistful) — 古琴《平沙落雁》, 0:10–2:50 of `data/bgm/pingsha.mp3` (source of the download not recorded).

# 印章 · seal (v3)

- **seal/huanghelou.svg** — 「崔顥之印」白文印. Glyph outlines from 崇羲篆體 Chongxi Small Seal (中央研究院 小學堂, 王心怡 书写),
  https://xiaoxue.iis.sinica.edu.tw/chongxi/ — **CC BY-ND 3.0 TW** (copy and use, commercial use included, with credit; the font
  must not be modified). The font is not shipped or altered: `tools/design-poem/seal.py` draws the four outlines into an SVG picture
  and adds the worn-stamp filter around them.
- **seal/baidi.svg** 「李白之印」 and **seal/chunwang.svg** 「杜甫之印」 — same font, same licence and credit, same script
  (`python3 tools/design-poem/seal.py <chongxi_seal.otf> baidi 李白` / `chunwang 杜甫`); all six glyphs are in the font.
